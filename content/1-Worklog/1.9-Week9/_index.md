### Week 9 Achievements:

* **Knowledge acquired:** I consolidated the complete Cloud Journey curriculum into one connected view of cloud foundations, migration, operations, security, modernization, containers, data, AI/ML, and professional sharing. I also learned to assess a solution through all six Well-Architected pillars and communicate the tradeoffs behind each decision.

* **Practical skills:** I mapped evidence to competencies, performed an architecture review, converted implementation notes into reproducible documentation, checked technical translations and learning posts, prepared a presentation narrative, and validated the full bilingual Hugo report.

* **Security, operations, and cost awareness:** The final review covered secret removal, least privilege, observability, recovery, rollback, resource ownership, reproducibility, cost notes, privacy, and cleanup. Temporary billable resources were inventoried and removed, while intentionally retained artifacts were assigned an owner.

* **Deliverables and evidence:** The final deliverables included a curriculum traceability matrix, evidence inventory, Well-Architected review, improvement backlog, reproducible runbook, privacy/secret check, completed workshop and blog materials, three Facebook posts, event reflections, presentation narrative, successful bilingual build record, cleanup inventory, and organized handover archive.
