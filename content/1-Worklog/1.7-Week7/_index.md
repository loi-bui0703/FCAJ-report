### Week 7 Achievements:

* **Knowledge acquired:** I understood the complete analytical lifecycle from source ingestion and governed S3 zones to catalog metadata, transformation, optimized querying, and decision-focused visualization. Metadata, data quality, security, lineage, retention, and cost were treated as core platform requirements.

* **Practical skills:** I designed a data-lake layout, ingested and cataloged data, profiled and transformed a dataset with Glue, generated Parquet partitions, optimized Athena queries, and published a QuickSight dashboard whose values were reconciled with source queries.

* **Security, operations, and cost awareness:** The platform used encryption, blocked public access, least-privilege permissions, named data ownership, separated rejected records, monitored ETL runs, controlled query results, measured bytes scanned, and cleaned up paid services. Quality and access tests were included alongside performance checks.

* **Deliverables and evidence:** The week produced a data architecture diagram, zone and naming standard, governance checklist, ingestion record, catalog schema, transformation and quality mapping, Glue job evidence, optimized SQL query set, performance/cost comparison, published dashboard, validation record, and cleanup inventory.
