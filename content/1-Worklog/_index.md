This worklog records how I completed the nine-week FCAJ internship from **15/06/2026 to 14/08/2026**. I followed the complete self-learning map from **[The First Cloud Journey](https://cloudjourney.awsstudygroup.com/)**, combined its hands-on workshops with official AWS documentation, and verified each topic through practical exercises, review notes, and resource cleanup.

The weekly contents are organized as follows:

**Week 1:** [AWS Foundations: account security, cost control, IAM, VPC, EC2, storage, databases, monitoring, and CLI](1.1-week1/)

**Week 2:** [Migration to AWS: 7 Rs, VM Import/Export, schema conversion, AWS DMS, and disaster recovery](1.2-week2/)

**Week 3:** [Cloud Operations and Infrastructure as Code: Systems Manager, CloudWatch, Lambda automation, CloudFormation, and CDK](1.3-week3/)

**Week 4:** [Security and Reliability: federation, permissions boundaries, Security Hub, KMS, AWS Backup, and network integration](1.4-week4/)

**Week 5:** [Containers and Continuous Delivery: Docker, Amazon ECR, ECS, Fargate, EKS, and CI/CD](1.5-week5/)

**Week 6:** [Application Modernization and Serverless: microservices, messaging, Lambda, API Gateway, DynamoDB, Cognito, and observability](1.6-week6/)

**Week 7:** [Data and Analytics: S3 data lake, Glue, Athena, Kinesis, governance, and QuickSight](1.7-week7/)

**Week 8:** [AI/ML and Generative AI: SageMaker AI, feature engineering, model deployment, Amazon Bedrock, and RAG](1.8-week8/)

**Week 9:** [Capstone, Sharing, and Handover: Well-Architected review, documentation, technical sharing, report validation, and cleanup](1.9-week9/)
