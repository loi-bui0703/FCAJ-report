### Week 4 Achievements:

* **Knowledge acquired:** I connected identity guardrails, centralized findings, encryption, audit evidence, backup validation, and network segmentation into one defense-in-depth model. I can distinguish preventive, detective, responsive, and recovery controls and explain how measurable RTO/RPO support reliability decisions.

* **Practical skills:** I tested federated and bounded access, triaged Security Hub findings, protected data with KMS, traced API evidence, evaluated WAF controls, created and restored a backup, and compared peering with a transit topology. Each exercise included a validation step, not only configuration.

* **Security, operations, and cost awareness:** Temporary credentials, least privilege, separation of key duties, documented exceptions, encrypted recovery points, restore testing, segmented routing, and removal of test resources were applied throughout. I also considered the operating and cost differences between point-to-point and hub-and-spoke connectivity.

* **Deliverables and evidence:** The week produced an access-guardrail matrix, access lifecycle, security-finding runbook, remediation evidence, KMS key-policy record, CloudTrail query result, backup/restore test report, network topology comparison, and prioritized security/reliability review.
