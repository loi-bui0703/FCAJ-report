### Week 8 Achievements:

* **Knowledge acquired:** I understood the full ML lifecycle from problem framing and feature engineering through training, tuning, evaluation, deployment, monitoring, and retirement. I also distinguished predictive-model workflows from generative-AI applications and learned how retrieval quality, attribution, and access control affect RAG reliability.

* **Practical skills:** I prepared data with Data Wrangler, trained and tuned XGBoost, evaluated multiple error measures, deployed and tested a SageMaker endpoint, and designed a Bedrock RAG application with explicit evaluation cases and source-based answers.

* **Security, operations, and cost awareness:** The work considered PII, least-privilege data access, artifact traceability, endpoint encryption, monitoring, drift, rollback, prompt injection, authorized retrieval, human review, token usage, and resource cleanup. Paid training and inference resources were removed immediately after evidence was saved.

* **Deliverables and evidence:** The completed outputs included an ML problem statement, metric rationale, data-flow definition, feature dictionary, quality report, training/tuning records, error analysis, selected-model card, endpoint validation and cleanup record, RAG architecture, evaluation matrix, guardrail checklist, risk register, and improvement backlog.
