### Week 5 Achievements:

* **Knowledge acquired:** I understood the complete container delivery chain from source code and Docker layers to an immutable ECR image, ECS task or Kubernetes pod, service discovery, load balancing, scaling, and controlled deployment. I can distinguish ECS and EKS responsibilities without treating either platform as only a container launcher.

* **Practical skills:** I built and scanned an image, deployed multi-service workloads on ECS, ran Fargate tasks, tested scaling and blue/green behavior, operated a Kubernetes deployment on EKS, and delivered an application revision through CodePipeline and CodeBuild.

* **Security, operations, and cost awareness:** The solution used non-root containers, runtime roles, restricted network paths, health probes, logs, immutable image identity, scoped pipeline permissions, failure testing, and explicit rollback criteria. I monitored capacity overhead and deleted high-cost cluster, load-balancer, and pipeline resources after validation.

* **Deliverables and evidence:** The week produced a Dockerfile and Compose configuration, ECR scan record, ECS task and service definitions, request-path diagram, scaling and deployment observations, Kubernetes manifests, RBAC notes, pipeline configuration, build/deployment logs, image-digest trace, functional test results, and cleanup checklist.
